CACH AP DUNG
  1. Dong NetBeans (hoac Clean & Build sau khi chep xong).
  2. Giai nen file zip nay de len thu muc project VanVaPhu (cung cap voi src/, nbproject/).
  3. NetBeans: Clean and Build. Chay: chuot phai MonitorServer.java > Run File, roi SensorLauncher.java > Run File
     (hoac Run Project - main class da duoc sua thanh coldstorage.server.MonitorServer).
  LUU Y: config.properties duoc dat lai kho dong = -18 ~ -15 (ban cu la -16 ~ -15 nen kho dong bao dong lien tuc).
         File log cu (logs/Kho___ng_A.log ...) giu nguyen; log moi ghi vao file co ten khong dau, vd Kho_Dong_A_528330cc.log.

DOT VA LOI (2 loi logic)

1. "Nhiet do dinh diem" trong Lich su su co sai
   - Truoc: updateAlertPeak() chi goi khi co REMIND (sau 5 phut vuot nguong) nen su co ngan chi ghi nhiet do luc bat dau.
   - Nay: SensorHandler goi updateAlertPeak() o MOI goi WARNING that. DatabaseUtil so sanh do lech so voi dai an toan
     cua kho (cao hon max thi cang cao cang te, thap hon min thi cang thap cang te), chi ghi khi te hon dinh hien tai.
     Co cache trong bo nho (OPEN_PEAK) nen khong query DB o moi goi; tu doc lai tu DB neu khoi dong lai giua luc su co dang mo.
   - Da kiem tra: -19 -> -25 -> -23 -> -17 (kho dong -18~-15) ghi peak = -25.0; su co am len -10 -> -8 -> -12 ghi peak = -8.0.
   - Doi chu ky ham: updateAlertPeak(ten, nhiet_do, safeMin, safeMax) (bo tham so isDong).

2. Nut "Gui thu" Telegram tu y bat thong bao that
   - Truoc: goi ConfigUtil.setTelegramConfig(true, ...) -> ghi enabled=true xuong config.properties.
   - Nay: TelegramNotifier.sendTest(token, chatId) gui truc tiep bang gia tri tren form, KHONG ghi config va khong phu thuoc
     o "Kich hoat". Chi nut "Luu cau hinh" moi thay doi config.

DOT VA LOI 6 LOI CON LAI (4 loi du phong + su co mo coi + xoa kho)

DU PHONG (backup/*, util/BackupUtil, util/ProductUtil, util/ConfigUtil, util/DatabaseUtil)
  1. Nguong cu o tien trinh du phong: BackupLauncher goi ConfigUtil.reload() + ProductUtil.refresh() MOI VONG (10s).
     Neu doc DB loi -> GIU snapshot cu (truoc day reload() thay bang snapshot RONG -> moi kho bi ap nguong chung sai; ap dung ca cho Server).
  2. DB khoa lam luong du phong chet: DatabaseUtil.getWarehouseState() tra ACTIVE / INACTIVE / UNKNOWN (loi doc). Chi dung luong khi INACTIVE.
     queryActiveWarehouseTypes() va getRecentPrimaryLogs() NEM SQLException (khong tra rong) -> launcher giu nguyen cac luong, client bo qua vong.
     Them PRAGMA busy_timeout = 8000 (Server va BackupLauncher la 2 tien trinh cung mo 1 file .db).
     Launcher tu dung lai luong da tu dung khi kho duoc dang ky lai. Da bo: isWarehouseActive(), getLastLogExcludingPrefix().
  3. Trung ma cam bien du phong: backupSensorId() = BACKUP-<toi da 12 ky tu doc duoc, bo dau tieng Viet>-<10 hex SHA-256 cua TEN DAY DU>.
  4. Nguong 20s co dinh: nguong = max(20s, 3 x chu ky gui THUC TE), chu ky = trung vi khoang cach 8 goi that gan nhat (bo khoang lang > 10 phut).
     Cung cong thuc voi OfflineTracker cua Server (MIN 15s) nhung MIN cao hon 5s -> du phong luon tiep quan SAU khi Server bao mat tin hieu.

SU CO MO COI + XOA KHO (util/DatabaseUtil, util/AlertStateTracker, server/MonitorServer)
  5. Khoi dong Server: DatabaseUtil.reconcileOpenIncidents() -> kho CON hoat dong: su co moi nhat duoc NOI TIEP (AlertStateTracker.resume),
     nen khong mo su co trung va goi SAFE dau tien dong DUNG su co cu; su co cu hon cua cung kho (mo coi tu loi truoc): dong,
     ket thuc = luc su co ke tiep bat dau; kho DA XOA: dong, ket thuc = lan cuoi cam bien that gui du lieu.
     Neu Server tat lau roi bat lai, thoi luong su co bao gom ca khoang Server tat (khong co du lieu de biet chinh xac hon).
  6. Xoa kho: dong moi su co dang mo (closeOpenIncidents), xoa trang thai AlertStateTracker + TrendTracker (tao lai kho trung ten se bao START dung).
     Neu tick "xoa luon lich su": xoa ca lich su su co (alert_history) cua kho do.

CON TON TAI (chua sua): isReplay loai goi hop le den tre giua cac luong xu ly song song (canh cua thiet ke, it gap voi chu ky vai giay).
